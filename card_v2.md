**Title:** Land-Use Change and Ecosystem Service Value in Khagrachhari, Chittagong Hill Tracts (2000–2025)

**Summary:** A 25-year Landsat and Google Earth Engine study maps land-cover change in a forested hill district of Bangladesh and values its ecosystem services with a condition-adjusted equivalent-factor method. It also models how that value flows from rural upazilas to the urban core across rugged terrain. A manuscript is in preparation; results will be shared after publication.
